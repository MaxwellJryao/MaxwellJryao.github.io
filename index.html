About

I am a second-year CS PhD student at the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Tong Zhang. My research focuses on reinforcement learning and large language models, with particular interests in autonomous agents learning, model reasoning, and their interdisciplinary applications. Before my PhD, I received my B.Eng. from Yao Class (IIIS) at Tsinghua University. I interned at Amazon, Ubiquant Hedge Fund, etc.
